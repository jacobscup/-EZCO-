# Ezco Project Roadmap

## 🎯 Vision

To become the most trusted platform for real estate transactions between property owners and Nigerians in the diaspora, powered by blockchain technology.

## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- Smart contract vulnerabilities → Regular audits, extensive testing
- Scalability issues → Optimize gas, consider L2 solutions
- Regulatory changes → Legal consultation, compliance monitoring

### Market Risks
- Low user adoption → Community building, strong value proposition
- Competitive threats → Innovation, superior UX, network effects
- Liquidity issues → Multiple payment options, partnerships

### Operational Risks
- Key person dependency → Team expansion, documentation
- Fund depletion → Strategic fundraising, profitability planning
